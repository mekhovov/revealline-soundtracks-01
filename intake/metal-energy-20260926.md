# High-energy metal and synth audition intake — 26 September 2026

This intake prepares eight complete, independently licensed recordings for hosted technical review. It does not grant listening approval, game admission, or default-playlist status.

The metal side targets the characteristics requested for RevealLine: driven riffs, clear rhythm sections, action-game pacing, industrial or synthesized texture, and stronger contrast than the backup metal auditions. The electronic side targets fast racing/action synth with persistent bass and drums rather than sparse chiptune.
