Licensing Info

Unless you buy an extended license (in which you can use the music without attribution) all music is royalty free and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, abbreviated CC BY 4.0

Hate reading long formal chunks of text? Here's a video explaining everything.

My music is in the Smart Content ID database, which means that if you're downloading the music for free, proper attribution is required in the description of you video.

The system scans the video description only, so this is where you need to attribute/credit. It scans for certain keywords, and credit should look like this:

Music: [song name] by Alexander Nakarada
https://creatorchords.com
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0

Replace [song name] with the actual song you're using. If you're using multiple of my songs, just write Music by Alexander Nakarada

If you purchase an extended license, you will be prompted to fill out a form which lets us whitelist your channel for the track(s) you've purchased a license for.

At this point, attribution is no longer required.

For Patreon subscribers, we whitelist the entire channel, which means they can use the entire library, indefinitely without attribution, as long as they are Patrons.

The platforms affected are YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. Twitch and X is not affected. For YouTube Shorts and TikTok please use their built in library (my music is available there).

Enterprise vs Personal/Small Business License

Personal License

The Personal License is the most common license and is suitable for individuals, content creators, freelancers, indie developers, and small businesses. It covers social media content, YouTube videos, podcasts, websites, games, commercial projects, and most other common uses.

Enterprise License

The Enterprise License is intended for larger organizations and productions that require company-wide licensing, additional legal documentation, or procurement compliance. Typical examples include television productions, radio advertising, film productions, major software publishers, and AAA game studios.

Both licenses grant the same broad usage rights. The Enterprise License simply provides a more formal license agreement tailored to corporate and broadcast environments.

What happens if you don't credit?

At this point your video will be ineligible for monetization, so please remember to credit if you monetize your content!

If you forgot to attribute when uploading the video, you will receive a claim. You can then add credits and fill out this form. That will make the system rescan your video description and release the claim if attribution is found.
