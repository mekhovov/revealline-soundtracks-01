They’re Going Down – Game OST Pack by YannZ

Fast. Loud. Vengeful. This high-energy soundtrack was originally made for the 2025 Pixel Art Game Jam title The Dead Guy That Made More Guys Dead — a brutal, retro-inspired top-down shooter about one very dead guy and a whole lot of other soon-to-be-dead guys.

Designed for games with tight pacing, combat momentum, and mood shifts, this OST pack blends djent metal riffs with atmospheric ambiences and a hint of cinematic dread.

Whether you're making a pixel-splatter arena brawler or just need to rip and tear with groove, this one’s got you.

TRACKLIST – They’re Going Down (click to preview)

🎧 Pixel Damnation (02:19) Loopable Combat Theme – 6-string distorted bass, electric guitar, punchy drums. Comes with a short 0:04 intro tag to help ease the transition from ambient zones.

🎧 Living ScornLiving Scorn (02:26) Modular Exploration Theme – SWAM strings, synthetic drones, wind textures, insects, and slow cymbal swells. Creepy and cinematic. Ambient versions included.

🎧 Revenge’s Waiting (00:48) Boss Fight Loop – A heavy, slow-groove loop in 12/8 with a foreboding edge. Short and loop-ready for mini-boss or climactic encounters.

Loop & Atmosphere Design

Pixel Damnation

  • Orchestration: 6-string bass, guitar, drums, strings, synths

  • Feel: Aggressive, dirty, tightly looped

  • Use: Standard combat zones or fast-paced encounters

  • Extra: Includes 4-second intro tag for smooth ambient-to-action transitions

Living Scorn

  • Orchestration: SWAM strings, Omnisphere pads, LABS & Splice textures

  • Feel: Hollow, tense, modular

  • Use: Ambient build-ups, level intro sequences, or suspense moments

  • Extra: Includes layered ambient stems and alternate mixes for dynamic implementation

Revenge’s Waiting

  • Orchestration: Guitar, bass, drums, synths, strings

  • Feel: Doom-y, spacious, off-kilter

  • Use: Boss fights or pacing shifts

  • Extra: Time signature in 12/8 for a fresh contrast to the main combat loop

Implementation Tips

You can use these tracks in a linear or adaptive setup:

  • Linear: Intro → Combat → Boss or combat + plot

  • Adaptive: Trigger Pixel Damnation from Living Scorn using intro tag

  • Use Revenge’s Waiting to break flow and raise stakes mid-combat or during key transitions

A [Living Scorn – Exploration Ambience]     B [Pixel Damnation – Combat Theme]    C [Revenge’s Waiting – Boss/Climax Loop]      A <--> B <--> C
