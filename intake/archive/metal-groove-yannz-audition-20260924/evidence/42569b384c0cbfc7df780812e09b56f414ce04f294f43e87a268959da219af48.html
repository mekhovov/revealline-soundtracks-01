The June 2023 Ko-Fi supported gift!

With this track, I was feeling an itch to make somethin' more aggressive. I've got all these sweet sounding guitars and amps and I don't use them enough! I also thought it would be interesting to hear a deep blend of metal and chiptune, so here's what that sounds like! Enjoy!

 

Achilles (Deluxe) includes:

- The full song

- Full loopable version + 14 mini loops

- Instrument stems

- 5 themed jingles

- 2 "Connectables" (Intro + outro)

- .OGG files of everything for easy importing!
